## 简体中文

### StitchRow 隐私政策

StitchRow（以下简称「本应用」）是一款纯本地的编织行数计数器。我们高度重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理（实际上是不处理）数据。

**1. 我们不收集任何数据**

- 无账号体系：无需注册、登录，我们不掌握任何用户身份信息
- 无埋点统计：不集成任何第三方分析 SDK，不记录使用行为
- 无广告、无内购、无订阅：不集成任何广告或支付 SDK
- 零系统权限：不申请通知、麦克风、相册、定位、蓝牙、通讯录等任何系统权限，运行时不会出现任何权限弹窗
- 永不出声：本应用不发声、不震动打断之外的任何打扰（触觉反馈可在设置中关闭）

**2. 付费说明**

- 本应用为 App Store **付费买断制**下载：一次购买，解锁全部功能，无任何内购、订阅或后续收费
- 购买与支付由 Apple 处理，本应用不接触你的支付信息；购买记录由 Apple 掌握，与本应用无关
- 退款请通过 Apple 官方渠道申请（reportaproblem.apple.com）
- 导出与导入功能对已购买用户完全可用，无任何付费墙

**3. 数据仅存储在你的设备上**

- 你的编织项目（项目名称、行数、循环与目标设置）与偏好设置（外观、触觉、常亮）仅以本地文件形式保存在设备上
- 本应用不进行任何网络请求，任何数据都不会离开你的设备
- 仅当你主动使用「导出」功能时，项目数据才会以 JSON 文件形式通过系统分享面板离开应用——导出与导入永远免费，无任何付费墙
- 卸载本应用即彻底、不可逆地删除全部本地数据

**4. 儿童隐私**

本应用不收集任何用户的任何数据，包括儿童。

**5. 政策变更**

若未来版本引入需要更新本政策的功能（如广告），我们将在本页面更新内容并标注新的日期。

## English

### StitchRow Privacy Policy

StitchRow (the "App") is a fully local row counter for knitting and crochet. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the App handles (i.e., does not handle) data.

**1. We collect no data**

- No accounts: no sign-up or login; we hold no identity information
- No analytics: no third-party analytics SDKs, no usage tracking
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions: no ad or payment SDKs integrated
- Zero permissions: the App requests no system permissions (no notifications, microphone, photos, location, Bluetooth, or contacts) and shows no permission dialogs
- Never makes a sound: the App is silent by design; haptic feedback can be turned off in Settings

**2. Paid app**

- The App is a **paid, one-time purchase** on the App Store: pay once, unlock everything — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no future charges
- Purchases and payments are handled by Apple; the App never touches your payment information, and purchase records belong to Apple, not the App
- For refunds, use Apple's official channel (reportaproblem.apple.com)
- Export and import are fully available to all buyers, with no paywall

**3. Data stays on your device**

- Your projects (names, row counts, repeat and target settings) and preferences (appearance, haptics, keep-awake) are stored as local files on your device only
- The App makes no network requests; no data ever leaves your device
- Only when you actively use "Export" does your data leave the App as a JSON file via the system share sheet — export and import are always free, with no paywall
- Uninstalling the App permanently and irreversibly deletes all local data

**4. Children's privacy**

The App collects no data from any user, including children.

**5. Changes to this policy**

If future versions introduce features requiring policy updates (e.g., ads), we will update this page with a new date.
